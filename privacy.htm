Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 15, 2025

Thank you for using HanziSpeak, a VR educational experience designed to help users learn Chinese characters through speech recognition.

1. Information We Collect

HanziSpeak does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app processes microphone input locally on your device solely for real-time speech recognition purposes. No audio data is uploaded or stored externally.

2. Microphone Access

The app uses the microphone to recognize spoken Chinese characters. This data is processed in real time on your device and is never transmitted or saved outside your device.

3. Analytics and Tracking

HanziSpeak does not use analytics services, trackers, or third-party cookies.

4. Data Sharing

We do not share any user data with third parties. Since we do not collect any data, there is nothing to share.

5. Children’s Privacy

HanziSpeak is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the app’s functionality changes. If so, we will update the effective date above. This page will always reflect the current policy.
